Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Project operator: nikolosbrand1-crypto. Contact: project support. Issues are public; never submit credentials or private information.

Local data

The desktop app processes video and transcription locally. Videos, transcripts, clips, metadata, caches, and processing records may remain on your computer until you delete them. There is no project-operated cloud storage service for these files.

Optional external processing

When an AI provider is enabled, transcript text and prompts may be sent to the selected provider, such as Google Gemini or OpenAI, for highlight selection and metadata generation. Source and speech-model downloads contact their respective providers. Their own privacy policies apply to received data.

Publishing accounts

Authorization tokens and account information are used to identify your chosen destination and upload selected content. TikTok basic profile and upload permissions identify your account and send reviewed clips to TikTok. Video and caption data go to the selected platform when you initiate an upload. The platform controls data already uploaded under its own policies.

Storage and removal

Publishing tokens and credentials are stored locally using Windows user-bound encryption, not in this website repository. Revoke access in the platform settings to stop authorization. Delete local credentials, caches, and output files to remove local records. Delete already uploaded content through the platform itself. Contact project support for assistance without posting secrets.

This website

This static site has no application login, analytics script, or video upload form. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process connection information under GitHub's privacy policy.
